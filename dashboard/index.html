How the model works

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chain

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category, computed from this snapshot at run time (at least 4 samples, else the global median), clamped to 0.04–0.90. A category whose tokens trade richly against TVL implies a richer launch.
  • Growth — 1 + clamped 7-day TVL change (−50%…+100%) × 0.6. Growing TVL lifts the implied value; shrinking TVL cuts it.
  • Maturity — 0.7 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 × 0.3. A protocol that ran for years with no token yet has had longer to build a launch. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chain — 1 + 0.03 per extra chain, up to 4 extra chains. A multi-chain footprint widens the launch.

What counts as a launch candidate

A protocol ranks only when it records no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and is not an exchange, a versioned pool, or a bridge / liquid-staking / restaking product, and its name carries no wrapped / staked / staking word and does not start with an exchange name. That is the watchlist's own rule, reused for the whole board — the watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling.

Farm plans

Every row carries six steps: a fresh wallet, the position sized by TVL ($50–$250), one qualifying action a week, a hold of 30–60 days by age, a claim check on the protocol's own page, and a farm log. Each step states what it costs and how long it takes.
