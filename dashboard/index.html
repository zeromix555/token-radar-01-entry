How the model works

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × heat × maturity × chain

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category, computed from this snapshot at run time. Categories with at least 4 samples use their own median outright; thinner categories are pulled toward the global median in proportion to their sample count — the global median counts as a prior worth 16 observations, so one sample moves the multiple less than 6% of the way, and a category with no traded protocol at all stays on the global median. Clamped to 0.04–0.90. A category whose tokens trade richly against TVL implies a richer launch.
  • Growth — 1 + clamped 7-day TVL change (−50%…+100%) × 1.0. Growing TVL lifts the implied value; shrinking TVL cuts it. At this weight the middle 80% of rows span ×0.87–×1.06, so growth actually ranks instead of rounding to noise, while the clamps still bound the extremes (a +4,448% week counts as +100%).
  • Heat — a second growth signal, at the category level: the median 7-day TVL change of the token-bearing protocols in the same category, measured against the market that week (the global median change). The snapshot's week fell 9.3% on median, so the relative reading is what separates categories that held up (Basis Trading, RWA, about 9 points above the market) from ones that fell harder (Liquid Staking, about 4 points below it). Heat is 1 + clamped relative change (−20%…+30%) × 1.0, pooled like the multiple, so a one-week category wobble moves a row at most about a tenth either way.
  • Maturity — 0.7 + sqrt(min(age, 1095 days) / 1095) × 0.3. A protocol that ran for years with no token yet has had longer to build a launch, but a year of waiting proves more than the third year does, so the curve flattens. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chain — 1 + 0.05 per extra chain, up to 4 extra chains. A multi-chain footprint widens the launch.
  • Confidence — each row says where its multiple came from: its category's own median (4 or more traded protocols), a pooled blend (1–3), or the global prior (none). A pooled multiple is an honest estimate, not a firm one, and the dashboard does not hide which rows carry it.
  • Category share — each row's share of its category's pre-token TVL, shown in the detail view. A protocol that is most of its category reads differently from one of 34 in it.

What counts as a launch candidate

A protocol ranks only when it records no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and is not an exchange, a versioned pool, or a bridge / liquid-staking / restaking product, and its name carries no wrapped / staked / staking word and does not start with an exchange name. That is the watchlist's own rule, reused for the whole board — the watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling.

Farm plans

Every row carries eight steps: a fresh wallet, a check of the protocol's own official channels before any money moves, the position sized by TVL ($50–$250), a category-specific weekly action, a hold of 30–60 days by age, a claim check on the protocol's own points page, an exit plan with its unwind trigger decided in advance, and a farm log. Each step states what it costs and how long it takes.
